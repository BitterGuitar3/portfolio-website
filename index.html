About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew — a gameplay programmer with a background in computer science and a passion for designing systems-driven gameplay experiences.

My primary interests are gameplay systems, AI behaviors, player mechanics, and modular game architecture. I enjoy building systems that are flexible, maintainable, and capable of supporting meaningful gameplay depth. Whether working in Unity or Unreal Engine, I focus on creating mechanics that are both technically sound and enjoyable for players to interact with.

Much of my experience comes from developing independent projects that challenge me to solve gameplay and systems-design problems. My most recent release is a modular chess prototype published on itch.io, built around a custom chess engine and runtime rules system. The project allows players to modify traditional chess mechanics through gameplay modifiers, creating unique combinations and alternative ways to play while maintaining a stable underlying rules framework.

In addition to my independent work, I have contributed to collaborative indie game development efforts, helping implement player-controller functionality and foundational AI systems while working alongside designers and other developers. These experiences strengthened my ability to translate design concepts into gameplay features and collaborate effectively within a development team.

Whether you're a recruiter, developer, or fellow creator, feel free to reach out. I'm open to opportunities that allow me to grow, contribute, and build something players will love.
