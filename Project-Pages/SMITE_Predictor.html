About

The whole idea behind this project is to be able to predict the chances of a team in SMITE winning by taking data in about the stats of the players, the stats of the gods and the stats of the common items the players would normally buy for a god and putting them against another team with their information. This is a project my brother really wants to do but unfortunately has not been able to contribute to due other priorities in life. We also have hit a roadblock in the development in the project with not having access to APIs and other information to make up to date predcitions. On my side of things, I have other projects I am working on, but plan on making some progress here and there on the end prodcut of this. If not to completion, to at least learn something new.

This predictor offers the user a selection of 24 gods that were available during the close beta of SMITE 2 with their original SMITE 1 stats (as I myself didn't have access as to it). Like the game, each team cannot have a duplicate god, meaning the whole team cannot play Loki as an example. The idea is that this list will update itself if and when Hi-Rez adds other gods as this selection dropdown is created by the webpage calling the server to fetch all the gods in the database. AFter the user selects the team compositions, they can submit the selections, which leads to the webpage calling the server for the information of each god that is made in the selection to use their stats to make a very rouch prediction of who will win. Currently it only uses the stats of the level 1 version of all the gods. The predictor simply takes the teams average stats and compares it to the other team. If it beats the other team in a category, the result variable ticks in theri favor by 1. Vice versa if they lose. After all the calculations, depending on which side the variable favors (postive for team 1 and negative for team 2), the page will print a message saying who is more likely to win.

What I Learned

Like I said, this is a rough prototype of the general idea we want to pursue. Its far from perfect, but it gets the general idea across. In the pursuit of making this prototype. I learned plenty. I atually started working on it before this portfolio. I had to relearn the basics of CSS, HTML, and Javascript. Afterwards, I had to figure out how to set up a basic database that can be used to retrieve the data. This biggest challenge of all was learning about the server communication between the front-end website and the database in the back-end. It is a differnt way of thinking as I am used to things executing in order. But when I was making the prediction calculations part, I kept running into issues with executing certain code too soon as the server is taking time to respond to the API requests from the site, resulting in errors or calculations not acting acordingly. Eventually, I figured things out with the help of online resources.

While this project may have not gone terribly in depth or isn't very complex, it at least exposed me to the basics of networking, which will be a challenge on its own for me later down the road when I start trying to make a game multiplayer. I know it will be necessary to look into sooner than later for the WW2 FPS shooter game that I am working on with a friend as it's end goal is to be a multiplayer game.
