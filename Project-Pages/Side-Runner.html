About

Side-Runner is a fast-paced, side-scrolling endless runner where players must time their jumps to avoid obstacles. The project introduced animation systems, particle effects, and audio — significantly enhancing the moment-to-moment feel of gameplay. It was part of the Unity Learn Junior Programmer Pathway and helped solidify key interactive and audiovisual systems.

Features and Mechanics

  • Core Controls:
    • Press Spacebar to jump
    • Press Spacebar again mid-air to double-jump (reduced power)
    • Hold S to double the game speed for added challenge
  • Game Loop: Colliding with an obstacle results in a game over. The player can restart with a single button press.
  • Visual & Audio Feedback:
    • Dynamic animations for movement, jumping, and crashing
    • Background music and sound effects for jumping and collisions
    • Particle effects (e.g. dust while running, explosions on impact) enhance feedback

What I Learned

This project introduced several new Unity systems and techniques

  • Background Looping:
    • Used a clever technique where two repeating background sprites shift left and reset position, creating an illusion of infinite scrolling without constant spawning or destroying objects.
  • Unity Animator System:
    • Learned how to:
      • Set up an Animator component with an Animator Controller and Avatar
      • Use parameters to drive transitions between animation states
      • Adjust animation speed dynamically via script
    • Gained experience managing transitions for player states like running, jumping, and colliding.
  • Particle Effects:
    • Integrated looping particle effects for running, and one-time burst effects for crashes.
    • Learned how to trigger effects via code (Play(), Stop()), and configure them to loop or play once based on context.
  • Audio Systems:
    • Implemented background music through the main camera's audio source.
    • Controlled sound effects (jumping, collisions) through the player script.
    • Gained an understanding of the roles of Audio Listeners vs Audio Sources, and how to manage them in code.

Reflection

Side-Runner marked a key point in my Unity learning where gameplay systems started to feel "alive" — with audio, animation, and effects coming together. It taught me how technical implementation and player feedback combine to elevate even simple mechanics into engaging experiences.
