About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew, a computer science graduate and programmer. My game development journey began in 2022, but after graduating in December 2023, I took some time away from coding and game development until late 2024. Around October/November of 2024, I started getting back into the swing of things by relearning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build my portfolio and collaborate on a project with my brother. After finishing my portfolio in January 2025, I began diving into Unreal Engine by exploring its documentation and free courses, while balancing a part-time retail position.

In early 2025, I discovered an exciting opportunity with an indie game development company through a community Discord server. After reaching out, they were impressed by my motivation and eagerness to learn, which led to me joining their team as one of their two main programmers, focusing on player interaction and gameplay in Unity.

Alongside my work at the indie studio, I've started a long-term project with a friend. We're both learning new tools—he with Blender, and I with Unreal Engine—and together, we're building the foundation of a first-person WWII shooter. Right now, I'm working on the basic player controls, movement mechanics, and gunplay, slowly piecing together a prototype as we go.

My main focus at the moment is my role with the indie game company, where I'm involved in shaping the core gameplay experience using Unity. When I have spare time, I continue to explore Unreal Engine and work on my personal project.

I’m always eager to learn and take on new challenges. Feel free to browse my portfolio, and don't hesitate to get in touch if you'd like to collaborate or discuss new opportunities!
