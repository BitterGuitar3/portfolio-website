About

Sky Cleaner is my first fully functional and complete game that I ever created. Back around October of 2022, I decided to start learning game development on my own through Unity and their online website Unity Learn. On this platform, they provide several different learning paths that users can go down to learn how the game engine works as well as develop different skills in the world of game development. After completing the Unity Essentials pathway, I immediately dove right in to the Junior Programmer pathway.

While going through the Junior Pathway, the instructor has you take small steps to buidling your own independent project starting from planning to prototyping to slowly add more detail with new models and coding until eventually you have your own project fully devloped. When I started my planning, I thought of something simple. I chose to make a Galaga like game and chose to make it simpler than the actual game as I was still learning and developing.

Features and Mechanics

This game contains basic movement mechanics. The player simply must use WASD to move around and the spacebar to shoot, however, the player can only have 3 bullets exist on screen at one time. A small detail I included when making the movemnet was the plan will tilt a little bit to the left or right when heading in those directions to simulate what a plane may actually do when flying. The propeller also has a little animation where it spins and the plane also has a clear shadow on the ground to make the played feel as though they are high off the ground, not to mention the clouds on the sides that also act as borders for the player.

The game also features 3 different difficulties, easy, medium, and hard. These difficlty settings determine the spawn rate at which the various enemies spawn that the player needs to avoid. The enemies spawn slightly off screen at a random horizontal postions, other than the big groups of enemies that spawn, that creates a more randomized and dynamic playing expereince. The purpose of the game is to obtain the highest score you can while avoiding the enemies. To obtain points towards your score, you must shoot or hit baloons that spawn randomly as you fly around. If you hit the baloon, you get 3 points, but if you shoot them, you get 5 points. Its a pretty simple concept just like Galaga. When the player collides with an enemy, the plane get destroeyd and a partical effect is played that simulates an explosion going off. It also triggers a sound effect that sounds like an 8 bit bomb went off.

Lastly the game contains several different sound effects. Whenever the player shoots at all, a little PEW sound gets played. The enemies also play a sound when they get hit, sadly it isn't the sound of a bird or dragon like I would want. The music in the background was chosen as I found it nice and simple, something that compliments the game itself.

What I Learned

In the process of going down the Junior Programmer pathway, I learned a lot more about the unity engine when it came to programming. Though the game is pretty basic, it was a bit of a long journy to get the the completion of the project and the pathways consdering I was still in College at the time. I learned the basics of C# and I also was able to understand the concept of OOP a bit better. It gave me an easier way to visualize classes, objects, and polymorphism and how the objects in the game interacted with each other through code by passing variables and showing how public and private variables work. I already had somne experience coding in my life before this, but I persoanlly feel that using Unity and haveing things be a bit more visual helped solidify my conception and understanding of the foundations of coding in general.

Potential Fixes and Updates

While developing the game, I've thought of several ways to buid upon the game. The first of many is making the difficulty of the game gradually get harder. For instance, on easy, have the game start spawning a couple smaller enemies and over time have its spawn frequency inncrease. Maybe even have it spawn bigger and stronger enemies. On harder difficulties, I should have different harder enemies spawn instead of having just an increased rate of spawns. I could also increase the speed of the enemy and the player to increase difficulty of control and avoidance of the enemy.

Another idea I had was to incorporate abilities. Like the player shoots an enemy or something and like a shield pickup ability spawns the player could use or even double barrels. Just adding something small to shake things up. These abilities and pickups wouldn't be permanent as if they piled up, it would make the game too easy. I also thought of allowing the game to save the top 10 high scores like an arcade machine so that way the player has some score they are trying to beat. I would make sure there are separate score boards for the different difficulties. Last change I though of was making the scene change dynamically. What I mean by that is allowing the time of day to change slowly while the game goes on. The background coule also change from the forest like background to something urban. Originally I planed for the background to be a more mountainous terrain that you could see the 3D aspect of it, but I could not find anything to my liking on the Unity Asset Store and I do not have the technical ability to make such art.
