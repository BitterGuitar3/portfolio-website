About

Sky Cleaner is the first complete game I developed independently using Unity. I started the project while working through Unity's official “Junior Programmer” learning pathway in late 2022 on their Unity Learn website. The course gradually guided me from planning and prototyping to full implementation, and this game was the result.

Inspired by classic arcade shooters like Galaga, Sky Cleaner is a simplified take on the genre that allowed me to apply foundational Unity concepts, experiment with core gameplay loops, and complete a full project cycle from start to finish.

Features and Mechanics

  • Movement & Shooting: The player uses WASD to fly a plane and spacebar to shoot, with a 3-bullet cap to encourage timing and movement. The plane tilts visually as it moves, with spinning propeller and shadow effects to enhance immersion.
  • Enemy Spawning & Difficulty: Three difficulty levels adjust enemy spawn frequency. Enemies appear in randomized horizontal positions just off-screen, creating varied gameplay each run.
  • Scoring System:Players earn points by popping balloons — more points for shooting than colliding. Score is tracked during gameplay to promote replayability.
  • Sound & Visual Feedback: Basic sound effects are implemented for shooting, hits, and explosions. Background music was selected for its retro feel and simplicity.

What I Learned

This project was pivotal in reinforcing my understanding of Unity and C#. I gained hands-on experience with:

  • Object-oriented programming (OOP) in a game context
  • Scene organization, input handling, and Unity's component-based architecture
  • Visualizing class-object relationships and understanding how data flows between components. It also helped me develop the patience and structure needed to carry a project from prototype to final polish while balancing my college coursework.

Potential Fixes and Updates

While functional, this project still has room for improvement

  • Dynamic Difficulty: Increase difficulty progressively by introducing tougher enemies and scaling enemy speed.
  • Abilities and Pickups: Add power-ups like shields or double-barrel shooting, with limited durations to maintain challenge.
  • Score Saving: Implement persistent high-score tracking with separate boards per difficulty level.
  • Visual Variety: Add dynamic background changes or time-of-day transitions to enhance visual interest during longer sessions.
