About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew, a computer science graduate and programmer. My game development journey started in 2022, but life events led to a few breaks along the way. After graduating in December 2023, and taking some time off due to circumstances, I've been getting back into the game development scene by working on my portfolio and collaborating on projects with others.

I've been working with Unity since September 2022, with a focus on gameplay mechanics and developing prototypes as well as learning about the engine and its capabilities. My background in computer science provides a strong foundation in problem-solving, algorithms, and software development. Recently, I have been revisiting Unity to clean up my projects, improve my codebase, and prepare my portfolio for future opportunities. I'm also excited to explore Unreal Engine once my hardware can support it, particularly for an upcoming project with a friend.

Currently my focus is on refining the projects I've already worked on and showcasing them on my portfolio. I'm cleaning up my code, commenting it, and putting everything on GitHub for easier access. While I don't have new project plans in Unity at the moment, I'm always open to prototyping if a new idea comes to mind.

I'm particularly excited about learning Unreal Engine for future projects. A friend and I have plans to develop a project using Unreal, but at the moment, my hardware is limiting my ability to dive into it. Once I upgrade my setup, I'm eager to explore Unreal's capabilities and apply it to more complex projects.

Feel free to browse my projects and get in touch if you'd like to collaborate or discuss any opportunities. I'm always looking for ways to expand my skills and take on new challenges!
