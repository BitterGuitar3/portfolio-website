About Me

Hi, I'm Andrew — a game developer and programmer with a background in computer science and a passion for creating interactive, systems-driven gameplay experiences.

My journey in game development began late in 2022. Since mid-to-late 2024, I've focused on building real-world skills through collaborative indie projects and self-driven learning. I currently serve as a gameplay programmer in a remote indie studio, where I take design documentation and turn it into player mechanics and in-game features using Unity. The project is fully asynchronous, and I communicate regularly with leads for clarification, asset coordination, and integration support with the other programmer.

In parallel, I'm independently developing a prototype for a first-person tactical shooter in Unreal Engine 5. This personal project focuses on realism, immersive systems, and responsive gunplay. I'm currently working on mechanics such as healing, reloading, and AI combat behaviors, using Unreal's Blueprint system.

I specialize in gameplay programming — creating systems that feel good to use and add meaningful depth to the player's experience. I'm comfortable working independently or in small teams, and I'm always eager to learn new tools and workflows.

Whether you're a recruiter, developer, or fellow creator, feel free to reach out. I'm open to opportunities that allow me to grow, contribute, and build something players will love.
